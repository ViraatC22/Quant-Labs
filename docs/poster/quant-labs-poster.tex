% ============================ LEFT COLUMN ===================================
\begin{column}{0.29\textwidth}

  \begin{sectionblock}{Problem}
    Trading evidence is scattered across executions, screenshots, notes, PDFs,
    scripts, and journal entries. Current tools track trades, but they rarely
    explain \textbf{why} a decision worked or failed.

    \vspace{1.5mm}
    \begin{itemize}
      \setlength{\itemsep}{1mm}
      \item Patterns disappear after the trading day.
      \item Rules live separately from the trades they affected.
      \item Review questions need evidence, not memory.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{sectionblock}

  \begin{sectionblock}{Approach}
    Quant Labs captures evidence, generates metadata, connects it to trades and
    strategies, and keeps the result searchable inside a local-first workspace.

    \vspace{2mm}
    \begin{softblock}
      {\bodyfont\bfseries\color{primary}Capture \(\rightarrow\) Enrich \(\rightarrow\) Connect \(\rightarrow\) Review\par}
      \vspace{0.8mm}
      {\smallbodyfont\color{muted}
      Links and uploads become structured research records with tags, setup
      notes, entry rules, exit rules, and risk context.\par}
    \end{softblock}
  \end{sectionblock}

  \begin{sectionblock}{Example Review Questions}
    \questioncard{Why did my VWAP pullback fail after a loss?}
    \questioncard{Which sources support or contradict this ORB rule?}
  \end{sectionblock}

  \begin{sectionblock}{Vault Capture}
    \screenshotbox[width=\linewidth,trim=0 2280 0 0,clip]{app-vault-capture.png}
    {\captionfont\color{muted}
    A link or file upload is transformed into auto-filled fields, generated
    tags, and strategy information for later graph search.\par}
  \end{sectionblock}

  \begin{sectionblock}{Generated Output}
    \begin{softblock}
      {\bodyfont\bfseries\color{primary}Auto-filled fields\par}
      \vspace{0.8mm}
      {\smallbodyfont\color{muted}
      Title, source type, extracted body text, source tags, generated tags,
      setup notes, entry rules, exit rules, and risk context.\par}
    \end{softblock}

    \vspace{1.4mm}
    {\captionfont\bfseries\color{primary}Example generated tags\par}
    \vspace{1.2mm}
    \tagpill{accent}{strategy:orb}\hspace{1mm}
    \tagpill{accent2}{entry:range-break}\hspace{1mm}
    \tagpill{warning}{risk:predefined-stop}

    \vspace{2.2mm}
    \begin{softblock}
      {\bodyfont\bfseries\color{primary}Strategy info created from evidence\par}

      Paper-test stricter opening-range breakout rules with relative-volume,
      time-of-day, and stop-placement filters before any live automation.\par}

  \begin{sectionblock}{Why It Matters}
    Quant Labs makes review reproducible. A trader can move from a vague
    question to the exact trades, notes, and sources that support the answer.

    \vspace{1.4mm}
    \begin{itemize}
      \setlength{\itemsep}{1mm}
      \item TradingView becomes an input, not the whole product.
      \item The durable asset is the trader's connected memory.
      \item Explanations stay tied to evidence instead of guesses.
    \end{itemize}
  \end{sectionblock}

  \begin{sectionblock}{Paper-Only Safety}
    The MVP is intentionally bounded: it captures, organizes, and explains
    evidence before any live automation is allowed.

    \vspace{1.4mm}
    \begin{softblock}
      {\bodyfont\bfseries\color{primary}Local-first by default\par}
      {\smallbodyfont\color{muted}
